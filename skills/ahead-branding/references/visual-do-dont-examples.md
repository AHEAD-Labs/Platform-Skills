# Visual Do's and Don'ts — Approved Examples

Use this reference when creating a PowerPoint deck or standalone AHEAD visual. The registry remains authoritative for live templates, tokens, logo files, and asset paths; this guide adds the approved visual examples and maps them to the source deck's slides.

## Do's

- Use the correct primary or secondary AHEAD logo and preserve clear padding around it.
- Use the approved brand fonts; Office deliverables use Aptos (2026 Style Guide). The example deck may still show Tenorite, its 2024 font.
- Use approved primary and secondary colors with accessibility color pairings in mind.
- Use the branded rounded rectangle and pill shape without elongating or changing their proportions.
- Use lines with rounded ends and a 1–1.50 weight depending on the use.
- Use the correct branded arrowhead format.
- Prefer abstract, minimalist visual treatments and limit the number of icons; avoid literal or overly detailed technology icons.

## Don'ts

- Do not use photography full-bleed from edge to edge of a slide.
- Do not use full-colored backgrounds besides gray or navy.
- Do not use bold or italicized headers.
- Do not use sharp-angle shapes such as triangles, sharp-cornered rectangles, or pentagons.
- Do not use dotted lines made of little squares.
- Do not use the standard logo against dark or blue backgrounds; use only the full-white knockout logo there.
- Do not use purple anywhere (the only exception is the deck template's own visited-link color).
