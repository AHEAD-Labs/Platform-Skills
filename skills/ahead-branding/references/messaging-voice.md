Apply to every piece of AHEAD copy, in every asset type.

**Status:** Brand confirmed on 2026-11-11 that this messaging, positioning, and voice guidance is current. It originated in the 2024 guide, and the 2026 upgrade builds on it.

## Naming and categorization

**Acceptable descriptions** — choose based on audience:

- Systems Integrator (SI)
- IT Solutions Provider
- IT Services Company
- Technology Solutions Partner
- Managed Services Provider (MSP)
- Cloud Solutions Provider

**Always emphasize:** speed + impact.

**Never use.** These are hard failures the validator checks for:

- VAR / Value-Added Reseller — **we are not a VAR**
- reseller
- distributor
- box pusher
- hardware vendor

If a source document or user draft uses one of these, replace it and say what you changed.

## Voice and tone

AHEAD is a trusted advisor and strategic partner. The tone should reflect that relationship: seek to educate, never patronize. Serious about the work and the expertise, while keeping a fun and positive perspective.

| Voice | Tone |
|---|---|

| Thoughtful | Active |
| Approachable | Inquisitive |
| Genuine | Client-centric |
| | Good-humored |

## Writing mechanics

- Use the **Oxford comma** throughout.
- **Minimize exclamation points.** Reserve them for genuine emphasis, which is rare in business writing.
- Technologies are common nouns: "cloud computing," "observability," "generative AI."
- AHEAD practices are proper nouns: "Cloud Practice," "Security Practice," "Intelligent Operations."
- Write "AHEAD" in full capitals. Never "Ahead," "ahead," or "AheadIT."
- Prefer active voice and concrete outcomes over adjectives. Confident, not boastful — let the outcome carry the claim.
- Lead with the conclusion. Slide titles and section headings state the claim; the body proves it.
