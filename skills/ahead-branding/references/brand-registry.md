## 1. Color palette (2026)

Source: `AHEAD_StyleGuide_2026`. Most applications use one or more primary colors.

| Ice Blue | `#E8F1FC` | 232, 241, 252 | 10, 2, 0, 0 | 656 |

### Neutrals

White `#FFFFFF` · Ice Blue `#E8F1FC` (tables, panels, separators; the 2026 light neutral) · Charcoal `#2A2A2A` (body text on light) · Dark Gray `#333333` (captions) · Footer Gray `#8C9BAB`.

### Accessibility pairings

Text contrast of at least **4.5:1** is required. The guide's pairing grid (checked with the WebAIM contrast checker) says which foreground sits safely on which background; "text" means safe as normal text, an asterisk means large bold text or an icon only (this includes the "E" in the logo). Read the grid in the guide (Color Palette → Accessibility) before pairing any foreground and background you have not used before; do not guess a pairing from memory. Large text is 14pt bold or 18pt and up.

### Gradient

Monochromatic. Use **no more than about 5% of the full gradient**, and **never the center**. There is **no fixed angle**; the 2024 rule of 69° is retired. In decks, **always use the published gradient image file** for the theme (§4). Never recreate a gradient.

### Chart series order

Electric Blue `#0B52ED` → AHEAD Blue `#009FDC` → Impact Green `#33CC99` → Navy `#123A61` → Coral `#FC794D` → Sky Blue `#9CD3FF` → Pale Teal `#ABEEE6`. *Confirmed by the Brand Team in the 2026 guide; treat as source of truth.* Light Orange and Ice Blue may be used if more series are needed.

### Accent — Brand Team use only (carried over from 2024)

`#9DE1F7` · `#99E2CC` · `#EB6056` · `#EC8058` · `#FCCA49` · `#333399` · `#ECA154`. The 2026 guide does not restate accents. Do not use them in a generated deliverable. Do not use purple anywhere.

### Stale 2024 palette

`#1C4CBF` Bright Blue · `#00B388` Impact Green · `#67C3E9` · `#6ECEB2` · `#006666` · `#102033` · `#5663C0` · `#1A4344` · `#1369CE` · `#ECF1F3`. Not allowed in new work; the validator fails them. Replace with the 2026 equivalents.

The general template's theme ("AHEAD - 2026", the Dusk style; the same theme is in CoBrand) is approved as is (skill owner on behalf of Brand, 2026-11-11). Use its colors freely in decks built on it; they arrive with the template's layouts and designed elements.

### Palette composition rules

- Always include a primary color.
- Do not use all light colors or all dark colors from the same region of the palette; it waters down brand recognition.
- Two to three colors is the norm for one treatment.

## 2. Typography (2026)

| Role | Typeface | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Everything users create** (decks, documents, spreadsheets, HTML pages, dashboards, emails, infographics, diagrams) | **Aptos** | The only typeface for materials AHEADians create with Claude or Glean. Light, Regular, Semibold, Bold and italics; **Regular** by default |
| Backup | **Verdana** | Only when Aptos is unavailable (for example, a web page on a device without Aptos) |
| Marketing-owned brand typeface | **Parabolica** | Owned by Marketing and used **primarily for marketing materials**: campaigns, advertising, print, signage, events, and swag produced by or with Marketing. Do not use it in user-created materials unless the user is producing a Marketing deliverable and says so |

Brand guidance, 2026-11-12: *"The skill should only use Aptos or Verdana (as backup) for materials created by users. Parabolica is primarily a marketing-owned font and should be used primarily for marketing materials."*

Reference sizes from the guide: header Regular 45pt, subheader Light Oblique 38pt. Italics may be used in a treatment, but **isolated italicized text should not be the header**.

**Stale:** Tenorite (2024 Office typeface), Poppins (2024 brand typeface), and PT Serif (2024 secondary) are no longer named in the guide. The validator fails them. Do not use them. Do not mix more than two font families in one document. Maintain a consistent heading hierarchy.

| Variant | File | Use on |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary logo (navy)** | `AHEAD-Logo-WEB.png` | White or light backgrounds and light images. First choice |
| **Reversed logo (white)** | `AHEAD-Rev-Logo-WEB.png` | Dark colors or dark images. **Never on a teal background** |
| **Alternate reverse logo** | see library | Only when the reversed logo cannot be used but there is still an opportunity to use navy |
| **Knockout logo (full white)** | `AHEAD-KO-Logo-WEB.png` | Only when the reversed logo cannot be used |

**Contrast:** the 2026 guide, confirmed by the Brand Team, lists `#FFFFFF`, `#0C243B`, and `#123A61` as the safe backgrounds for the logo. On `#0C243B` and `#123A61` use the reversed (white) logo, because the navy file does not read on navy. Never place the logo on low-contrast colors or on colors that fail the accessibility pairing grid.

**Do not:** rearrange the logo; use the "E" in other words or formats; change the logo color, even to another on-brand color; place it on low-contrast colors; use the "E" on its own (the website favicon is the only exception); place it on an angle; place it on another logo; place it on colors that fail accessibility tests.

**Clearspace:** no less than the height of the "E" between the logo and surrounding graphics or text. Exceptions: the tagline "Learn. Grow. Achieve." and appropriate background imagery.

**Pairing with another logo:** horizontally, at least the height of the "E" between the two. If the other logo is taller, center-align vertically. If shorter, bottom-align, and generally scale it up so it looks proportionate to the AHEAD logo.

The **AHEAD png Logos folder is the canonical source of truth for logos.** Always fetch from it when the runtime can. The two bundled files (`AHEAD-Logo-WEB.png`, `AHEAD-Rev-Logo-WEB.png`) are fallback copies only. The logo files carry transparent padding, so the visible logo measures about 0.57" × 0.12" when the picture is placed 0.57" wide.

**Resolution order when generating a deck programmatically (`scripts/ahead_pptx.py`):**
1. An explicit `logo_path` passed by the caller (use this for a freshly downloaded file from the AHEAD png Logos folder).
2. **Bundled fallback** — `assets/logos/AHEAD-Logo-WEB.png` (navy) and `assets/logos/AHEAD-Rev-Logo-WEB.png` (white).
3. If neither is available: proceed without a logo, insert nothing in its place, and state the gap in the brand report. Never substitute a different, unofficial, or hand-drawn logo. The AHEAD png Logos folder is the only source for logo files.

**Deck placement (2026 templates):** bottom-right, about 0.57" wide with aspect ratio locked, left edge about 9.09", top edge about 5.19". Full numbers are in `asset-powerpoint.md` and `assets/brand-tokens.json` (`deck_logo_placement`).
