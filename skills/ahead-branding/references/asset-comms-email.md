## Why this matters

Project, feature, and release communications are increasingly drafted in Glean and Claude and sent as-is. People open and act on a document that looks familiar. Familiarity is the goal here, not decoration.

## Interim structure for an employee communication

1. **Subject line** — plain, specific, outcome-first. No exclamation points. No "Exciting news!"
2. **One-sentence what-and-when** at the very top.
3. **What is changing** — short paragraphs, not a wall of bullets.
4. **What you need to do** — explicit actions with owners and dates.
5. **Why** — one short paragraph of context.
6. **Where to get help** — named person or channel.
7. **Signature** — approved AHEAD email signature from the Personal Branding → Email Signatures library.

Keep it scannable. Use one clear call to action.

## Formatting

- **HTML email:** Aptos with a Verdana fallback (`font-family: "Aptos", Verdana, sans-serif`). Many mail clients will render the fallback, which is expected and acceptable. Parabolica is Marketing-owned and is not used in user-created email.
- **Outlook / Office-composed email or an attached document:** Aptos.
- Body text charcoal `#2A2A2A` on white. One primary brand color for links, rules, and headings — usually AHEAD Blue `#009FDC` or Navy `#123A61`.
- One logo, at the top or in the signature. Not both, not repeated per section.
- No more than three brand element treatments. Most internal emails need zero.
- No background images behind body copy.

## Voice for internal comms

Educational, approachable, genuine, positive, active. Never patronizing, never breathless. Oxford comma. Minimize exclamation points. See `messaging-voice.md`.

## Review gate

A one-to-one or small-team message needs no review. **Any communication going to a department, a region, or the whole company is Evergreen** — flag Brand Team review in a chat note (brand@ahead.com), without adding a review block to the message, and route structure questions to Louise Thompson.
