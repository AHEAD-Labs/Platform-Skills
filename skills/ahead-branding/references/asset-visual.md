# Asset Rules — Infographics, Diagrams, Charts, Standalone Visuals

Triggered by: infographic, diagram, architecture diagram, process flow, journey map, chart, data visualization, social graphic, event graphic, banner, swag, poster, image.

Rules follow the 2026 Style Guide (Brand Elements, Photography, Events & SWAG).

## Composition

AHEAD style is clean and minimalist. **Maximum three brand element treatments per visual** — this includes ebooks, whitepapers, and slide graphics.

Brand elements: **pills, arrows, swooshes, tiles, icons, and shapes**.

| Element | Rules |
|---|---|
| **Pill** | **90° or 180°** only. Use **singly**: no overlapping pills, no groups. Fill: solid palette color, texture, or photography. Micro (buttons) or macro formats. Always the default button shape. Show the whole pill only when functional, such as a CTA |
| **Arrow** | Never points left. Right angles only, no diagonals. Fill: solid color or the gradient, **not photography** (exceptions only for specific campaigns). Singular; never overlapping or in groups (exception: building icons) |
| **Swoosh** | Fill: photography or a solid palette color |

- Apply shapes zoomed in, rarely showing the full shape. Use borders and page breaks as natural interruptions to the graphics.
- Prohibited: dashed or dotted strokes on brand shapes; 0%–100% gradient fill; sharp-angle shapes such as triangles or pentagons.
- Lines have rounded ends at a 1–1.5 weight.
- Prefer abstract, minimalist treatments and limit icons; avoid literal or overly detailed technology icons.

## Color

At least one primary color (`#0C243B`, `#123A61`, `#009FDC`, `#0B52ED`, `#33CC99`, `#FC794D`). Secondary colors (`#9CD3FF`, `#ABEEE6`, `#FFA26A`, `#E8F1FC`) when more are needed. Do not build a visual entirely from light colors or entirely from dark colors. No Brand Team-only accents. No purple. Text contrast at least 4.5:1; check unfamiliar pairings against the accessibility grid in the Style Guide.

**Gradients:** monochromatic, no more than about 5% of the full gradient, never the center, no fixed angle (69° is retired). For PowerPoint-style gradients use the published image files from the PPT-Gradients folder; never recreate them.

## Charts and data visualization

- Series order: Electric Blue `#0B52ED` → AHEAD Blue `#009FDC` → Impact Green `#33CC99` → Navy `#123A61` → Coral `#FC794D` → Sky Blue `#9CD3FF` → Pale Teal `#ABEEE6`. *Confirmed by the Brand Team in the 2026 guide.*
- Label directly on the data where possible; drop the legend when one series is shown.
- Gridlines light gray, thin. No 3D effects, no drop shadows, no gradient-filled bars.
- Font: **Aptos** for every visual a user creates, with Verdana as the backup. Parabolica is Marketing-owned; use it only for a Marketing deliverable.

## Photography

The goal of the 2026 approach is to show the **impact AHEAD is making**, not only what we do at a basic level (a hospital patient overcoming illness, rather than racks being installed). Source from the libraries in `brand-registry.md` §5 and choose by subject:

| Subject | Image type |
|---|---|
| Abstract topics such as AI or liquid cooling | Abstract images |
| Client impact, or the results of how our technology is used | Nature and real-world photos |
| Our technology, and nothing more | Data center and coding images |
| Executive briefings, or our people | Collaboration and people images |

Guidance:

- **Show people's faces.** Avoid obvious filters. If there is a dominant color, make sure it makes sense for the brand.
- Ask "does this look too staged?" before using an image. Every image choice should feel intentional.
- **Nature:** show nature within the context of people or society, with evidence of human impact. Tie it to the subject or to the words in the title (clouds for cloud storage, water for liquid cooling, a bridge for "bridge the gap", a trail for "chart a path").
- **Office and collaboration:** use AHEAD's own office shoots first, then stock. Avoid anything too stocky, generic, unrealistic, or with cheesy backlighting.
- **Abstract:** go for brighter, more illustrative styles. Avoid abstract buildings (a main brand element five or more years ago; it reads as a step backward) and anything not "futuristic" enough.

Not allowed:

- Artificial graphics or effects superimposed inside the image.
- Baked-in filters such as sepia or black-and-white. Stylized filters using brand colors are acceptable.
- A "stocky" feel — handshake-in-a-boardroom, wooden-blocks-spelling-CHANGE, glowing-padlock-over-a-laptop, stressed-people-in-a-meeting.
- Photos used as underlays when people are in the photo.
- Full-bleed photography edge to edge on a slide.
- **AI-generated imagery as representative on-brand photography.** AI imagery is acceptable only as an explicit example of AI output.

Photo imagery may be applied as cut-outs beneath brand element shapes. No other texture or treatment without consulting the Brand Team.

## Logo on imagery

Place the logo on a light, uncomplicated portion of the photo with sufficient contrast. Frame it within elements of the photo and the photo's edges. Crop or lighten the image if needed. Keep it proportional — a correctly placed but tiny logo is still a violation. Choose a corner. Never place it in the middle of the photo's focus, on a busy background, on a color that fails the accessibility grid, or on another logo.

## Events and swag

- Event materials must be on brand and recognizably AHEAD **beyond logo usage**. Official event materials are Marketing materials, and **Parabolica** remains their default font when Marketing produces them. When a user asks for an event graphic themselves, use Aptos and suggest Marketing for official event materials. The Brand Team may add fonts or colors while still following the usage rules; those additions are pending approval, so do not add them yourself.
- Swag must follow all brand guidelines and be approved by the Marketing Team. **Anything using the AHEAD logo must be approved by the Brand Team.** If the logo is not used, pre-existing Brand-designed logos and imagery are preferred, though not required.
- For merchandise rules and ordering, refer the user to the AHEAD Experience Marketing Guidelines 2023 and The AHEAD Store guide (links in `brand-registry.md`). Activities not listed in the merchandise guidelines are vetted through the Experience Marketing team.
- Events and swag are **always Evergreen** — route to brand@ahead.com.

## Generated images

If you generate an image for an AHEAD deliverable: never render the AHEAD logo or wordmark into it, never present it as AHEAD photography, and label it as illustrative. Prefer an approved photo or a brand-shape composition.
